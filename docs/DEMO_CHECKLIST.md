# Demo Checklist

1. Log in with a clean participant account.
2. Show Profile & Preferences and explain that filters can be changed temporarily.
3. Add pantry items with at least one near-expiration date.
4. Open Meal Recommendations and point out Smart Score, Pantry Match, Nutrition Fit, Use Soon, Still Needed, and Smart Swaps.
5. Demonstrate Top 10 and Show More.
6. Save one meal, skip one, and mark one as made.
7. Regenerate recommendations and explain adaptive learning and recipe fatigue.
8. Open Recommendation History.
9. Show the Admin Dashboard metrics without exposing participant credentials.
10. End with the research question: whether pantry-aware recommendations improve awareness, usefulness, and ingredient utilization.
